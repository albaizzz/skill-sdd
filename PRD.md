## 1. Overview
SDD SKILL is a set of skills and instructions for AI coding agents that helps engineering teams prepare and execute feature work through Specification-Driven Development. The workflow gives project context through `foundation-gate`, guides developers and product owners to author a complete 13-point feature specification with `sdd-specify`, then supports human-authored task plans that AI validates and executes only after approval.

## 2. Problem Statement
- AI-generated feature specifications can make it harder for developers and product owners to own the requirements and may introduce assumptions they did not approve.
- Developers and product owners need a structured way to write complete, reviewable feature specifications before implementation.
- AI should help validate specifications and execute approved tasks without taking authorship of the requirements or task plan.

## 3. Target Users
- Primary users: developers/programmers and product owners.
- Stakeholders: tech leads and engineering teams who review specifications and task plans.

## 4. Goals & Success Metrics
- Help developers/programmers, product owners, and tech leads create feature specifications to serve as the source of truth for AI-assisted implementation.
- Every specification covers all 13 points or marks an unresolved item as `TBD` with an owner.
- AI does not write or modify the human-authored 13-point specification.
- Task plans are written by humans, validated by AI against the approved specification, and approved before AI executes them.

## 5. Key Features / Functional Areas
1. `foundation-gate` — create and validate project foundation documents so AI agents can understand the project.
2. `sdd-specify` — guide a human author through a complete 13-point feature specification and validate its completeness.
3. `sdd-task` — let humans write a task plan, validate it against the approved specification, and let AI execute only approved tasks.
4. `AI Agent Advisor` — a clearly labeled appendix outside the 13 required points where AI may provide recommendations without changing the human-authored specification.

## 6. Non-Goals
- AI must not author or modify the user's 13-point specification.
- AI must not author the task plan; it may validate the human-authored plan.
- AI must not execute tasks before the specification and task plan are approved by a human.
- The `AI Agent Advisor` appendix is advisory and does not change the approved requirements.

## 7. Constraints
- Preserve a clear distinction between human-authored requirements and AI-generated advice.
- The specification must retain the 13-point structure defined by the project.
- Human approval is required before task execution.
- The existing repository currently has conflicting authoring instructions: `README.md:15` and `.claude/skills/sdd/SKILL.md:39-41` say the agent writes the specification, while `.claude/skills/spec-writer/SKILL.md:13-14` says the human is the author. Resolve this inconsistency through the approved SDD process.
